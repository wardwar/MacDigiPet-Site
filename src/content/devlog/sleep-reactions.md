title: Pixel ?, ! and … marks, and cleaner sleep
date: 2026-10-13
summary: Small emotes get their own pixel bubbles, and sleep text stays off the body.
---
Confused, stumbling and sulk-glance marks are now small pixel bubbles aimed at the crown. While the pet speaks they ride the corner of the reaction bubble.

![Sleep reaction under the feet](/img/sleep-reaction-under-feet.png)

When the native Z bubble shows, the text "Zzz…" is dropped, and other sleep reactions sit under the feet instead of over the body.
