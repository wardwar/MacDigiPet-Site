A round of small things that make MacDigiPet feel more alive on a real desk, plus a few clips filmed on a MacBook while testing the alpha.

## Nature calls

When your partner needs the toilet, it says *“I have to get back to the village!”* (the clip above shows the Indonesian version) and heads home instead of making a mess on your desktop. Sleepy partners do the same when it’s time for bed. It’s one of the DW1 habits that makes the village feel like a real home.

## Battles move into the notch

During a fight the notch now opens on its own and stays open until combat ends. It shows both fighters’ portraits, HP and MP, and lets you choose **Auto** or one of your partner’s three equipped techniques for its next attack. Press **A** or **1–3** to choose quickly. Lost HP stays white for a moment before the bar catches up, so every hit reads at a glance.

<video src="/media/battle-megadramon-greymon.mp4" poster="/media/battle-megadramon-greymon.jpg" muted loop playsinline controls aria-label="Megadramon and Greymon battling on the desktop"></video>

Macs without a notch get a virtual one in the centre of the menu bar, and the floating console is still there if you turn notch controls off.

## A sign of life beside the notch

The closed notch used to be a black shape you had to discover. It now shows your partner’s portrait on one side and its mood on the other, so you know it’s there and ready to use. Hover over it to open the controls.

## Original DW1 area music

You can now turn on background music from the menu bar. Eight original Digimon World 1 area themes are included, among them File City by day and by night, Misty Trees, Gear Savanna and Beetle Land. Each plays its intro once and then loops seamlessly. Pick one track, or let **All tracks** rotate through them, and set the volume from the same menu. It’s off by default.
