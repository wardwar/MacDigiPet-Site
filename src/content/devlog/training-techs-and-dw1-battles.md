A big week. Most of it went into the things you actually *do* with your partner: training it, teaching it techniques and sending it into battle. Everything here is part of the DW1-focused alpha build.

## Today's training plan

Training now lives in **Status → Training** as a simple icon menu using the original DW1 stat icons. Pick the stats you want to work on, set how many sessions you want today, and press **Start**. Your partner works through the plan on its own and shows what it's training next and how long is left.

Plans only last for today. Tomorrow your partner asks once before starting a new one, so it never trains for days without you noticing. Fixed cooldowns and quotas are gone: training now costs **energy**, the same energy that drives everything else, so a tired partner rests instead of grinding.

## Learning techniques the DW1 way

Partners now learn techniques like they did in Digimon World 1:

- **In battle** — when an opponent uses a technique your partner is compatible with, it may pick it up.
- **Through quiet practice** — Brains training and calm time at home can also unlock compatible techs.

Progress is saved, Brains gives a bonus, and repeated failed attempts don't wipe out what's been built up. When something new is learned, Status tells you.

<img src="/img/status-tech.png" alt="The Tech tab in Status with three equipped techniques and the 56-tech chart" width="340" style="display:block;margin:0 auto;max-width:100%;height:auto">

The new **Tech** tab shows the full 56-technique chart with the original specialty icons. Choose a slot, then a learned technique, to set the three moves your partner uses in both village and desktop battles.

## Battles that feel like DW1

Fights got the biggest upgrade:

- The original **BATTLE** intro, with its sound cues, and DW1 battle music.
- **HP above each fighter** and floating **damage numbers**.
- A **FINISH meter** that fills as the fight goes on. When it's ready, your partner glows with a finisher aura and can let loose its special move.
- A zigzag command strip in the notch with animated technique icons, plus a boost gauge.
- Original DW1 hit reactions and area effects are back.

## Smaller things you'll notice

- **Animated portraits** — 76 forms now use genuine two-frame V-Pet portraits in the notch and in Status.
- **Music controls in the notch** — play, pause, skip, choose a track and set the volume on any display.
- **Info & alerts** — care and setup warnings now show up in Status and the notch, with a direct button to fix village problems.
- **Saved generations** — continue with any archived partner, or delete one you no longer need.
- **Call Here** now works on every monitor.
- **More to say** — partners comment on harvests, training, finishers and what's going on around them, with a soft DW1 blip when they start talking.
- **Shadows** — every DW1 model now casts a soft shadow that matches the scene's lighting.
- A small confirmation sound on every action, and flying partners now stay in the air while idle.
