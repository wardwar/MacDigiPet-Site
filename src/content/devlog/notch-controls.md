---
title: Notch controls and a compact Prod menu
date: 2026-10-13
summary: Hover the MacBook notch to feed, call, visit the village or open Status.
---
Hovering the notch expands it into a partner glance with **Feed**, **Call here**, **Village** (with an inline place picker) and **Status**. Displays without a notch get a small pill in the menu bar centre.

![Notch controls](/img/notch-controls.png)

The Prod menu bar menu now keeps only settings, so day-to-day care happens at the notch. Dev keeps the full menu.
