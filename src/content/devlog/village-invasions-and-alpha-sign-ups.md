Every screenshot in this post is from the current alpha build, recorded on a real MacBook. Nothing is mocked up.

## Something's moving near our village…

Every so often, an opponent wanders into your village. It can also happen in the middle of your work. Your partner turns to you, says *"Something's moving near our village…"*, and runs home to deal with it.

<img src="/img/alpha-invasion-notice.jpg" alt="Garurumon on a Mac desktop saying something is moving near the village" width="720" style="display:block;margin:0 auto;max-width:100%;height:auto">

How often this happens is up to you: Relaxed, Normal or Active. You can also look for an opponent yourself in **Status & Care → Battle**. A sleeping, hungry or busy partner won't be pulled into a fight.

## The fight plays out on your wallpaper

The battle opens with the original DW1 **BATTLE** intro, right over the village. The notch turns into a compact battle HUD with HP, MP, your three equipped techniques and the **FINISH** meter. The fight itself stays on your wallpaper.

<img src="/img/alpha-battle-intro.jpg" alt="The original BATTLE intro over the village wallpaper as Garurumon faces PlatinumSukamon" width="720" style="display:block;margin:0 auto;max-width:100%;height:auto">

Opponents carry the floating DW1 HP bar, and every hit pops the original bouncing damage numbers.

## FINISH, then boost it

FINISH fills as the fight goes on. When it's ready, press **F** (or click FINISH). Your partner charges up, and pressing F again during the charge boosts the finisher. Then it lets loose: for Garurumon, that's Howling Blaster.

<img src="/img/alpha-howling-blaster.jpg" alt="Garurumon's Howling Blaster hitting PlatinumSukamon on the wallpaper" width="720" style="display:block;margin:0 auto;max-width:100%;height:auto">

Win, and your partner tells you *"The village is safe!"* and walks back to you. Lose, and it stays home to sulk, get back up and train a little.

Known issue: in the current build, the finisher aura can look like a flat yellow box instead of the DW1 flame effect. It's on the fix list.

## Closed-alpha sign-ups are open

The first alpha focuses on Digimon World 1 partners, care, evolution, the village and these desktop battles. We're picking a small group of testers first, in waves.

- **You'll need:** a Mac on macOS 15 or later, 15–30 minutes on about three days a week for 14 days, and honest feedback.
- **Played Digimon World 1?** That's a plus, not a requirement.
- **To apply:** join our Discord, read **#tester-info** and apply privately.

No build has gone out yet. Applying doesn't guarantee access, and selected testers get the build in Discord once final checks pass. [How testing works →](/beta)
