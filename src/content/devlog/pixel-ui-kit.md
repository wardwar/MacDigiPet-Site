---
title: A pixel UI kit for status, notch and bubbles
date: 2026-10-13
summary: System symbols and blurred capsules are gone. Everything now speaks the same pixel language.
---
The status panel, notch controls and reaction bubbles now share one pixel icon kit and V-Pet portraits.

![Status & Care panel](/img/status-panel.png)

- Portraits keep their recovered source pixel grid instead of being sampled down.
- Monochrome or blocky sources were replaced with colour sprites.
- Unreadable glyphs (meat, call, back, toilet, shield, broom, dumbbell, mood) were redrawn.
- The reaction bubble now points at the projected crown of the partner.

![Pixel speech bubble](/img/pixel-speech-bubble.png)
